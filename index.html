About HALA

HALA is a free navigation and map app developed by Shabplt System in Malaysia. HALA is built local-first: the places you save, the searches you make, the reviews and reports you add, and the settings you choose stay on your device by default. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics, or a server for your saved places. Community reports are an optional feature that uses Google Firebase/Firestore only after you turn it on.

This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it when you use the map, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

HALA stores the following locally in your browser or app storage:

  • Saved places, home and work shortcuts, and favourite stops.
  • Recent searches and recent routes.
  • Settings such as theme, units, language and voice preference.
  • Road reports you create and their expiry times, unless you opt in to community reports. If enabled, the device also keeps an anonymous Firebase ID and local copies of reports you shared.
  • Star ratings, reviews and compressed photos you add to places.
  • Offline map regions and cached routes you choose to download.
  • The last known position, so the map can reopen near you.

This data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Offline regions and cached routes can also be deleted inside the app.

Location

  • HALA asks for location only when you request it or start navigation. You can deny the permission and still browse the map and search for places.
  • While navigation is active, the app uses the device GPS stream to show your position and follow the route. In the current version, updates stop when the app is locked or backgrounded.
  • HALA does not upload a raw GPS trail or keep a location history on a server. It stores only the last position on your device.

Requests to map and routing providers

To draw the map, search for places and calculate routes, HALA sends requests directly from your device to third-party services. These requests include only what is needed to answer them — typically the coordinates or bounding box of the area, your search text, the route origin and destination, and standard technical information such as your IP address and device user agent.

  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles.
  • Photon (Komoot) — place and address search.
  • Overpass API — nearby places and speed-camera data.
  • Valhalla and OSRM — driving, walking and cycling routes, and speed limits derived from the route.
  • Transitous — public-transit routes. Transit requests send the origin and destination coordinates and the requested departure time.
  • Esri World Imagery — satellite tiles, only when satellite view is selected.
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons — place descriptions and photos, only when you open a place that has them, plus the image host credited for a photo.
  • OpenStreetMap API — details for the selected map element.
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google) — only when community reports are enabled.

These providers may log requests, including IP addresses, under their own privacy policies. HALA does not receive those logs and does not build a profile from them. Requests are made over HTTPS.

Road reports

Reports you create are stored on your device by default and are not sent to other people unless you enable community reports. Reports expire automatically. While community reports are on, you can delete your own shared report from the report detail sheet. When sharing is off, you can remove the local copy and turn sharing back on to delete the shared cloud copy. Clearing app data removes local data.

Community road reports (optional)

Community reports are off by default. HALA does not load Firebase or send Firebase requests until you enable the feature in Profile → Community reports.

When enabled, HALA silently creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication session. No email, phone number, password, or social account is requested. The app sends the report type, point location, optional bearing and note, timestamps, expiry, a coarse H3 area cell, and the anonymous Firebase ID to Cloud Firestore. Other HALA users who opted in can read active reports near them. Firebase/Google also receives standard request metadata such as IP address and user agent.

HALA does not upload a continuous GPS trail, speed history, route history, saved places, reviews, or photos. On first enable, active local reports from the last 24 hours may be uploaded; older reports stay on the device. If Firebase or the network is unavailable, reports stay local.

Turning community reports off stops all community reads and writes and hides shared reports on your device. Local copies remain. Reports you already shared stay visible to other opted-in users until they expire unless you delete them from your own report details. HALA keeps the anonymous session on the device so you can turn sharing back on and manage reports; clearing app data or uninstalling removes the anonymous ID and that ability.

Expired cloud reports may remain in Firestore storage because automatic TTL deletion is not enabled in this build. The app and security rules deny reads of expired reports. Operational cleanup removes them separately.

Photos and reviews

You can add a star rating, a short review and up to four photos to a place. Photos are compressed on your device before saving. Reviews and photos are stored only in the app's local storage and are never uploaded. This version has no community review server, so only you can see what you add.

You can edit or delete your review and delete photos in the place page, or remove everything by clearing app data. HALA reads only the files you explicitly choose in the system photo picker. It does not scan your gallery, request camera access on its own, or read photo metadata for analytics.

Donations

HALA may offer an optional "Support HALA" button. If you tap it, your browser opens a ToyyibPay checkout page. ToyyibPay handles the payment and its own privacy policy applies. HALA does not receive or store your card, bank or e-wallet details.

Voice guidance

Spoken directions use your device's text-to-speech engine. HALA does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

What HALA does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in. Community reports, when enabled, use an anonymous Firebase ID; there is no cross-device sync.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No background location tracking.
  • No automatic access to your contacts, photo gallery, calendar, health data or messages. Photos are read only when you pick them for a place.

Permissions the Android app requests

  • Location (approximate and precise) — to show your position, follow a route and search nearby.
  • Internet and network state — to load map tiles, search results and routes.
  • Vibrate — for navigation alerts.

Photo attachment uses Android's system photo picker. HALA does not request camera or storage permission; only the files you pick are read.

Children

HALA is a general-purpose navigation app and is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Delete your data

You can delete community reports and local HALA data as follows:

  1. Delete a shared report: while community reports are on, open the report in HALA and tap Delete this report. If sharing is off, turn it back on to delete the shared copy; otherwise you can remove only the local copy.
  2. Delete local data: open Android Settings → Apps → HALA → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall HALA. This removes saved places, searches, settings, local reviews/photos, local reports, the anonymous Firebase session, and cached data.
  3. Need help after clearing app data? Email aslamafendi@gmail.com with the approximate report area and time; we will delete the shared report.

Deleted: local HALA data (as above) and community reports you delete. Kept temporarily: reports you do not delete remain visible to opted-in drivers until they expire; expired cloud documents may remain in Firestore storage because automatic TTL deletion is not enabled, but they are excluded from all app reads. The anonymous Firebase ID is removed when you clear app data or uninstall. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to answer it. Because HALA has no user accounts, there is no personal profile to delete.

Changes to this policy

If HALA's data practices change, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. Community reports were added on 31 October 2026 as an optional, off-by-default feature.
